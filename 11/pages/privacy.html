Privacy Policy.

Information on your device

The site uses browser local storage only for your color-theme and layout-direction preferences. You can remove these values at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Forms & Enquiries

Contact, sign-in, sign-up, and newsletter forms are demonstrations. Submitted values remain strictly in the current page session and are cleared after successful client-side validation.

Third-party requests & Tracking

All scripts, icons, fonts, and images are stored locally in the website folder. The site makes zero analytics, advertising, tracking, or external CDN requests.

Your choices

You may use the site without completing any form. To reset appearance preferences, clear local storage for this local website origin.
