The planted tank method

Light, carbon,
nutrients—balanced.

Most algae problems begin when one input outruns the others. Start modestly, keep the schedule stable, and adjust one variable at a time.

  • 01
    Six-hour light windowIncrease only after plant growth is visibly established and clean.
  • 02
    Weekly water changeRefresh essential micro-minerals and reset organic waste buildup.
