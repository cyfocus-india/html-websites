Care before commerce.

Stillwater is a specialist store demo grounded in a timeless idea: livestock retail should begin with observation, transparent water parameters, and fewer impulse decisions.

The practice

We slow the handover down.

A healthy fish is only one part of a successful purchase. The new habitat has to be cycled, compatible, and maintainable too.

  1. 01
    Rest and observe New arrivals are quarantined, conditioned on high-grade diets, and monitored before joining the sales tanks.
  2. 02
    Share the water We publish exact pH, temperature, and hardness ranges with every species card.
  3. 03
    Ask about the tank Tank dimensions, maturity, existing tankmates, and adult size expectations come first.
  4. 04
    Follow the transition Acclimation instructions and a 14-day health promise ensure your setup succeeds long after handover.
“Good fishkeeping is mostly the habit of noticing small changes early.”

What we value

Stable basics over clever fixes. Captive-bred livestock where available. Plants that match the equipment. Honest “not yet” advice.

The counter rules

Six core commitments.

Ethics

Welfare first

We will decline a sale when a habitat is not ready or a species pairing is dangerous.

Provenance

Clear origins

Captive-bred, local farm, and sustainable wild species are labelled plainly with zero ambiguity.

Transparency

Useful honesty

Adult growth sizes, territorial behaviors, and demanding diets are never downplayed.

Longevity

Repairable tools

We prioritize hardware brands with accessible spare parts, seals, and rebuild support.

Open learning

Shared knowledge

Care advice and compatibility guides stay free, accessible, and scientifically grounded.

Sustainability

Lighter footprint

Consolidated insulated packaging, right-sized delivery routes, and recyclable materials.
