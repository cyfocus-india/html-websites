Cycling · 10 min

The invisible engine: your nitrogen cycle

How beneficial nitrifying bacteria convert toxic fish respiration into harmless plant nutrients, and how to verify biological stability.

  • Ammonia (NH₃): Converted by Nitrosomonas. Target: 0.0 ppm.
  • Nitrite (NO₂⁻): Converted by Nitrospira. Target: 0.0 ppm.
  • Nitrate (NO₃⁻): Plant uptake or weekly dilution. Target: <20 ppm.
  • Ready Benchmark: Dosed 2.0 ppm ammonia drops to 0 in 24 hours.
Protocol: Fishless Ammonia Dosing 3–5 Weeks
Stocking · 7 min

Why adult size matters more than shop size

Juvenile fish represent only 20–30% of their adult metabolic load. Plan swimming strata, territory, and school cohesion before purchase.

  • Biomass Growth: A 4″ adult creates 8× the waste of two 2″ juveniles.
  • Strata Separation: Surface gouramis, midwater tetras, bottom corydoras.
  • Schooling Minimum: Keep at least 8–10 fish for true schooling comfort.
  • Staggered Additions: Add 3–5 small fish every 14 days to prevent spikes.

Water · 6 min

A weekly routine you can actually keep

A structured 20-minute maintenance rhythm that protects biological stability without stripping the beneficial microbiome.

  • 1. Pre-Test (5 min): Log pH, temperature, and nitrate before opening tank.
  • 2. Plant & Glass Care (5 min): Scrape glass; prune yellowing lower leaves.
  • 3. 20–25% Siphon (7 min): Vacuum debris; refill temp-matched ±0.5°C.
  • 4. Media Rinse (3 min): Squeeze sponges in drained tank water only.
Cadence: 20-Min Weekly 20–25% Volume

Monthly planted-tank reset

Thirty quiet minutes.

  1. 01
    Observe before touching Note circulation patterns, leaf coloration, fish breathing rates, and areas collecting organic waste.
  2. 02
    Trim tall stems first Work from back to front so loose floating clippings stay easy to collect without tangling foreground moss.
  3. 03
    Open the current Thin crowded plant clumps around the filter intake and surface skimmer return.
  4. 04
    Change 30–40% water Siphon substrate debris, refill with matched temperature dechlorinated water, and dose liquid trace minerals.
