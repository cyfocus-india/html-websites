Our shelf test

Four questions before anything gets a price tag.

  1. Genuinely safe & useful: Does this solve a real animal need without causing stress or confusion?
  2. Transparent ingredients & origin: Can we trace exactly where it was made and what’s inside?
  3. Durable for real animal life: Will it survive enthusiastic chomping, mud, and multiple washes?
  4. Approved for our own pets: Would we proudly bring this home to our furry family members?
