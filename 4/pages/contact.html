Quick answers

Before you ask the cat.

Can my dog visit the store?

Absolutely! Friendly, leashed dogs are always welcome. We provide water bowls, treat samples, and ample room to explore.

How does curbside pickup work?

Place an order or call ahead. Pull into our designated pickup bay behind the store and we will bring your items directly to your vehicle.

Can you order my usual food recipe?

In almost all cases, yes! Send us your brand, specific recipe, and bag size and we will order it in with our weekly deliveries.
