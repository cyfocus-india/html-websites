Pocket guide · First 7 Days

The first-week rhythm.

Keep the world small while your new friend learns the sounds, scents, and routines of their new home.

  1. Establish one quiet retreat: Bed, fresh water, and a cozy den with zero surprise human traffic.
  2. Repeat predictable rhythms: Meals, bathroom breaks, naps, and gentle playtime at consistent hours.
  3. Expand in layers: Introduce one new room, visitor, or neighbor walk only after current spaces feel safe.

🩺 Health reminder: Sudden changes in water intake, appetite, or energy warrant a veterinary check. Our journal is for lifestyle support, not clinical diagnosis.

Label literacy

Four things worth checking.

1. Life Stage Statement

Look for the complete-and-balanced designation specifically matching your pet’s life stage (puppy, adult maintenance, or senior).

2. Named Protein Source

Look for explicit proteins (e.g. "Deboned Wild Salmon" or "Cage-Free Turkey") rather than generic meat by-product terms.

3. Realistic Feeding Guide

Feeding charts are baselines for intact working animals. Adjust down by 10–15% for indoor and neutered companions as needed.
