Terms of Service

Fair, clear rules for shopping, store visits, grooming appointments, and returns.

Effective date: August 2026

1. Shopping & Orders

All orders placed online or in-store are subject to product availability. We reserve the right to correct pricing or inventory errors prior to shipment or pickup fulfillment.

2. Delivery & Free Shipping

Standard neighborhood delivery is complimentary on all eligible orders of $45 or more within our local Portland delivery radius. Orders under $45 ship for a flat $4.99 rate.

3. 30-Day Joyful Returns

Unused gear, unopened toys, and clean beds in original condition may be returned or exchanged within 30 days of purchase with receipt. If your pet refuses a new food bag, bring it in for our Taste Guarantee store credit.

4. Grooming Room Safety & Cancellations

For the safety of all salon guests, current rabies vaccination records are required prior to grooming services. We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice for appointment rescheduling or cancellation.

5. Pet Wellbeing Disclaimer

Advice provided on our website, in our care journal, or by store associates is intended for lifestyle and nutritional guidance only and does not replace licensed veterinary medicine.
