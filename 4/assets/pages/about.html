Who We Are

About Us

Pawfolk began with a very itchy rescue dog, a cupboard full of confusing labels, and the conviction that caring for animals should feel warmer, simpler, and kinder.

Open since 2018

The short version

Built for real animal life.

Founder Jules Martin wanted one neighborhood haven where questions were warmly received, ingredient labels made sense, and nobody gave side-eye for bringing a nervous or reactive dog through the door.

Today, Pawfolk is a tight-knit collective of pet nutrition nerds, Fear-Free certified groomers, foster volunteers, and world-class ear scratchers. We stock selectively, learn constantly, and happily admit when a veterinarian is the right next call.

What guides us

Kind is a practical choice.

Clear Over Clever

Plain-language ingredient panels, honest recommendations, and zero manufactured marketing panic.

Useful Over Excessive

We would rather help you choose one truly great, durable item than five almost-right gadgets that end up in landfills.

Care Without Judgment

Every pet and every human arrives with a unique history and budget. We meet you where you are with kindness.

Community shelf

Good neighbors share.

Foster Starter Kits

Local foster volunteers receive 15% off first-week essentials, complimentary harness fit-checks, and free sample bags.

Open-Bag Donations

If your pet refuses a new food, suitable opened bags are safely repackaged and routed directly to our rescue partners.

Quiet Shopping Hours

Every Tuesday morning from 9am to 11am, we dim lighting and turn off music for sensory-sensitive pets and humans.
