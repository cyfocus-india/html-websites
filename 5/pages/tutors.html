Our faculty standard

Expertise is only the beginning.

How our teachers make a meaningful difference in every student’s learning journey.

01

Explain multiple ways

A truly effective explanation meets the student’s current conceptual model first, then systematically bridges to formal understanding.

02

Notice the thinking

Tutors look beyond simple right or wrong answers to diagnose the exact problem-solving method and logic that produced it.

03

Actionable feedback

Every piece of feedback specifies the immediate next step so students know precisely what skill to practice before the next session.
