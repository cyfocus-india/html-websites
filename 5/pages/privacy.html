Privacy notice.

A plain-language overview of how Northstar Tutoring handles, protects, and respects student and family personal information.

Educational Policy
Transparency first

1. Information we collect 2. How we use data 3. Student record safety 4. Your parental rights 5. Contact our privacy team

1. Information we collect

We may collect parent and guardian contact details, student grade level, enrollment records, batch attendance history, tutor lesson logs, submitted homework, mock test results, and diagnostic assessments. Payment card data is processed directly and securely by authorized PCI-compliant third-party providers; Northstar never stores full payment card numbers.

2. How we use data

Collected information is used strictly to provide personalized tutoring services, coordinate batch timetables, maintain student dashboard access, communicate weekly academic progress with families, ensure classroom safety, and fulfill educational compliance requirements.

3. Student record safety

Student academic and attendance records receive elevated privacy protection. Access is restricted strictly to the enrolled student, authorized parents/guardians, designated course tutors, and center academic directors who need it to support learning.

4. Your parental rights

Parents and adult students have the full right to review, request corrections to, export, or ask for the deletion of personal account records at any time, subject to essential tax, billing, and educational compliance records retention.

5. Contact our privacy team

For any inquiries regarding data protection, record requests, or our privacy practices, please contact our administrative office at privacy@northstartutoring.example.
