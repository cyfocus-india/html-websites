Terms of service.

Clear, honest expectations for student enrollment, batch attendance, learning materials, billing, and respectful classroom conduct.

Student Agreement
Mutual standard

1. Enrollment & Plans 2. Attendance & Absences 3. Tuition & Invoices 4. Student Portal Access 5. Classroom Code of Conduct 6. Policy Updates

1. Enrollment and learning plans

Enrollment is confirmed upon mutual agreement of batch schedules, grade placement, and completion of tuition arrangements. A learning plan represents our dedicated pedagogical roadmap, structured to foster mastery and study habits.

2. Attendance and cancellations

Regular attendance is fundamental to academic momentum. Families are requested to notify the center at least 12 hours prior to an absence. Make-up options or recorded revision materials will be coordinated based on weekly schedule capacity.

3. Tuition and billing

Tuition fees are billed monthly on the 1st of each calendar month. All course books, worksheets, mock exams, and digital portal features are bundled into the stated tuition without hidden fees.

4. Student portal

Portal login credentials are for the exclusive use of the enrolled student and their immediate family. Users agree to maintain confidentiality of passwords and immediately notify faculty of any unauthorized account activity.

5. Classroom code of conduct

Students, families, and tutors are committed to creating a respectful, supportive, and intellectually engaging environment. Punctuality, thoughtful participation, and mutual respect are expected in all in-person and digital classrooms.

6. Policy updates & inquiries

These terms may be revised periodically to reflect evolving academic programs. For any questions regarding terms or enrollment, please contact our administrative desk at hello@northstartutoring.example.
