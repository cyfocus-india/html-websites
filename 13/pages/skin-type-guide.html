Skin Type Guide

Understand your skin. Choose what’s best for it.

Everyone’s skin is unique. Knowing your skin type is the first step to building a routine that delivers real, lasting results.

Know Your Skin Type

Normal

Balanced, not too oily or dry.

Smooth texture Small pores Few imperfections Looks healthy

Oily

Shiny, prone to acne & enlarged pores.

Excess oil production Visible pores Acne & blackheads Shiny appearance

Dry

Feels tight, rough or flaky.

Flaky or rough patches Tight feeling Dull appearance May feel itchy

Combination

Oily in some areas, dry in others.

Oily T-zone Dry or normal cheeks Visible T-zone pores Breakouts in some areas

Sensitive

Prone to redness, irritation or reactions.

Redness or blotchiness Itching or burning Reacts to products Needs gentle care

Not sure about your skin type?

Look at your skin throughout the day or try our quick test.

The Bare-Faced Test

Wash your face and wait for 1 hour. Then check your skin.

Tight & flakyYou likely have Dry skin.
Shiny all overYou likely have Oily skin.
Shiny T-zone, dry cheeksYou likely have Combination skin.
Feels balancedYou likely have Normal skin.
Red, itchy or uncomfortableYou likely have Sensitive skin.
