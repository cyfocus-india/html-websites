Terms of use

This website is a front-end demonstration. Product names, inventory, prices, events, addresses, and contact details are fictional demo content unless replaced by the site owner.

No online purchase contract

The shopping bag, account forms, RSVPs, and enquiries do not create orders, reservations, or accounts. A production site must connect these experiences to secure services and confirm transactions separately.

Content and imagery

Original site imagery was generated for this template. The interface uses a locally embedded subset of Lucide icon geometry under its permissive license. See the project README for implementation credits.

Appropriate use

Do not use this template to misrepresent real inventory, pricing, event availability, privacy practices, or organisational identity. Replace all demo contact details before publishing.

Accessibility

The template is designed for keyboard access, reduced motion, responsive layouts, theme switching, and right-to-left presentation. Site owners remain responsible for testing any changed content and integrations.
