Our story

A game store should feel like an invitation.

Meeple & Marrow began with a simple belief: a thoughtful recommendation can turn an ordinary evening into a tradition.

“The best game on the shelf is the one your table asks to play again.”

We opened our doors in 2014 with three shelves, one long table, and a handwritten list of games we could teach without opening a rulebook. The shelves grew. The table stayed.

Today we carry modern strategy games, family favourites, card games, puzzles, and role-playing tools—but our work is still personal. We play what we sell. We ask who is coming over. We say when a game is probably not the right fit.

That honesty is how a shop becomes a community: one clear teach, one welcoming seat, and one memorable evening at a time.
