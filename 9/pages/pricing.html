Standard weekday daytime, evening, and weekend visits are billed at our regular flat rate. Nationally recognized holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day) are billed at time-and-a-half (1.5x) to appropriately compensate our dedicated frontline nurses and caregivers.

The Daily Care Package ($180/day) covers up to 12 continuous or split hours of care per day with a dedicated caregiver. It includes personal grooming, medication reminders, meal preparation, vital monitoring, and light mobility assistance at a discounted daily bundle compared to hourly billing.
