Costs and savings

Understanding the Numbers

Savings vary with your electricity tariff, daytime consumption, solar access and system size. Homes that use a large share of their energy during daylight often see the strongest benefit. Use our calculator for an initial estimate.

Cost depends on capacity, panel and inverter selection, roof structure, cable routing and safety requirements. Our listed product prices are indicative; your proposal follows a site assessment.

Many suitable residential installations target a broad three-to-six-year simple payback range, but the actual period depends on system cost, generation, tariff escalation and approved subsidy.

Eligible residential consumers may qualify under prevailing central or state programmes. Amounts and rules change, so SolarNest checks the current process when preparing your proposal.

System and installation

Your Rooftop System

The physical installation of a typical home system is often completed within a few days. Approval, material lead time and net-metering schedules may extend the overall project timeline.

A standard on-grid inverter shuts down during a grid outage for line-worker safety. Backup operation requires a compatible hybrid inverter and battery or another approved backup configuration.

A practical planning estimate is around 80–100 square feet of clear, largely shade-free area per kW. Roof geometry and module selection affect the final layout.

Panels still generate under diffused daylight, but output is lower than on clear days. Annual estimates account for typical local weather patterns.

Care and longevity

Long-Term Ownership

Periodic cleaning, a visual safety check and performance monitoring are usually sufficient. Cleaning frequency depends on dust, bird activity, rainfall and roof access.

Quality solar panels commonly carry long performance warranties and can continue producing for 25 years or more. Inverters generally have a shorter replacement cycle.

A properly engineered mounting system protects roof integrity. The assessment identifies waterproofing, structural and access considerations before installation.
