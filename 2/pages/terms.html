Service Terms

Terms

Service terms for DriveWay courses, bookings and student accounts.

Course bookings

A booking is confirmed after acceptance by DriveWay and any required payment. Students must provide accurate enrollment information.

Lesson changes

Lesson changes should be requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled start. Late cancellations may be counted as used sessions.

Safety

Students must follow instructor directions, carry required learner documents and avoid driving when impaired or medically unfit.

Payments and refunds

Package terms, inclusions and refund eligibility are confirmed at enrollment. Government charges are separate unless stated otherwise.
