Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your driving lessons.

How many driving lessons do I need to get my license?
Most beginner drivers need between 15 to 20 one-hour practical lessons to gain full confidence and clear the RTO driving test on their first attempt. Our Four-Wheeler Comprehensive package includes 20 structured sessions covering road basics, city traffic, parking drills, and two mock tests.
Do I need a Learner's License before booking lessons?
Yes, in accordance with transport regulations, you must possess a valid Learner's License (LL) to drive on public roads. If you do not have one yet, our front desk team provides full assistance with LL online application and RTO slot booking.
Can I choose between Manual and Automatic transmission?
Absolutely! We have dedicated fleets for both manual and automatic training. You can choose your preferred transmission during enrollment or switch anytime depending on your learning goals.
What is your lesson rescheduling policy?
We understand schedules change. You can easily reschedule or cancel an upcoming lesson through the online student dashboard up to 12 hours before your scheduled time slot at no extra charge.
Do you conduct mock driving tests before the official RTO exam?
Yes! All our comprehensive courses include 2 realistic mock driving tests graded on the exact parameters checked by RTO examiners (reverse S-bend, 8-track, parallel parking, and hill-start clutch control).
