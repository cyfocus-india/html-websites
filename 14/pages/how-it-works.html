Smart Move Preparation

Your Move Day Checklist

Follow our structured 3-phase checklist to make your relocation calm, organized, and effortless.

Phase 1

48 Hours Before Move

  • Defrost refrigerator and drain washing machine water pipes.
  • Set aside personal valuables, jewelry, laptops, and original documents in a personal bag.
  • Secure elevator booking permission & Society Gate Pass (NOC) from building management.
  • Disconnect TV setups and take reference photos of complex cable connections.
Pro Tip: Pack an essential "First-Night Box" with toiletries, chargers, bedsheets, and medication so you don’t have to unpack immediately.
Phase 2

On Moving Day

  • Walk through each room with the MoveMate Supervisor to point out high-fragility glassware.
  • Verify that room-wise color labels (Kitchen, Living, Master Bedroom) match the packing list.
  • Inspect your dedicated container truck before the tamper-evident serial seal is locked.
  • Receive your live GPS tracking dashboard link and the assigned driver’s direct contact number.
Pro Tip: Keep pets and toddlers in a quiet separate room or with family to prevent stress while heavy loading ramps are in operation.
Phase 3

At Your Destination

  • Confirm the container serial seal is completely intact before unloading begins.
  • Direct the movers to place labeled cartons and heavy furniture in their assigned rooms.
  • Inspect assembled beds, wardrobes, and electrical appliances with the supervisor.
  • Complete the digital inventory sign-off and download your official tax invoice receipt.
Pro Tip: MoveMate crews will reassemble all standard furniture and clear away empty carton debris before departing.
