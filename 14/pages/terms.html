Terms & Conditions

Welcome to MoveMate. By requesting a moving estimate, creating an account, or hiring our shifting and packing services, you agree to the following terms and operational guidelines.

1. Estimates & Binding Quotes

Online estimates calculated via our interactive calculator or web forms are indicative and based on standard volume averages. Final binding quotations are established following an in-person or video survey that verifies actual item counts, fragile requirements, access constraints, and optional services.

2. Booking Confirmation & Scheduling

A relocation booking is confirmed upon acceptance of the written quotation and receipt of any agreed scheduling advance. Move slots are subject to vehicle availability and municipal transit regulations.

3. Customer Responsibilities

  • Ensure accurate address details, elevator reservations, and parking permissions at both pickup and delivery locations.
  • Declare all fragile, high-value, oversized, or perishable items prior to packing.
  • Personally secure and transport all cash, valuable jewelry, official identity documents, deeds, and essential medicines.
  • Ensure an authorized adult representative is present during the entire packing, loading, and unloading process to sign the inventory sheet.

4. Transit Insurance & Damage Claims

MoveMate provides comprehensive transit protection for declared inventory packed by our verified crews. In the rare event of item damage during transport, claims must be logged within 48 hours of delivery along with photographic evidence.

5. Rescheduling & Cancellation

Shifting dates may be rescheduled free of charge with at least 48 hours notice. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled pickup time may incur a nominal crew preparation fee.

6. Contact & Support

For questions regarding terms, billing, or claims, please contact our support team at care@movemate.example or call +91 98765 43210.
