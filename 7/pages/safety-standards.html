Zero Compromise Safety

SAFETY IS OUR #1 PRIORITY

We adhere strictly to international play park guidelines (ASTM F1918 & BIS 15877) to ensure every minute of playtime is joyful, safe, and impeccably hygienic.

OUR 4 SAFETY PILLARS

BIS Certified & Flame Retardant Equipment

Every slide, ball pit border, climbing grip, and bridge is manufactured from high-density flame-retardant EVA foam and food-grade non-toxic HDPE plastics. No sharp metal edges or exposed bolts.

Inspected Weekly

Hospital-Grade Multi-Stage Sanitization

Our ball pits undergo automated ultraviolet (UV-C) ball washing twice daily. High-touch equipment is steam-sterilized with child-safe organic disinfectants every 3 hours.

UV-C Certified Clean

Certified First-Aid & CPR Staff

Our floor play coaches are certified by the Red Cross in Pediatric First Aid and CPR. We maintain an active 1:6 coach-to-child ratio in baby zones and 1:8 in senior obstacle zones.

Red Cross Certified

Matching Wristbands & 100% CCTV

Parents and children receive cryptographically paired RFID wristbands at entry. No child is allowed to exit the turnstile without their corresponding parent band being scanned.

Anti-Lost Protection
Hygiene & Traction Standard

ANTI-SKID GRIP SOCKS POLICY

For maximum hygiene and fall prevention, grip socks with rubberized bottom treads are strictly required for all children and adults entering the play arenas.

  • Prevents slippage on high-gloss slide dismount ramps
  • Protects sterile soft foam vinyl padding from dirt and debris
  • Available at reception in all children & adult sizes for just $3.50
