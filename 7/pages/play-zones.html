Developmentally Engineered Spaces

OUR 4 DEDICATED PLAY ZONES

Every zone is strictly age-segregated, ensuring that infants crawl peacefully while older kids conquer high-energy obstacles with zero collisions.

AGES 0 – 2 YEARS

BABY ZONE (SENSORY OASIS)

A calm, fully-padded sanctuary designed for crawlers, cruisers, and early walkers. Features non-toxic sensory tactile walls, low soft foam inclines, and an exclusive baby ball pool.

  • Low height foam mirrors & musical walls
  • Gated entry preventing older children from entering
  • Daily UV sterilized soft plush toys
  • Dedicated nursing and diaper care suite adjacent
Book Baby Session ($15) Safety Protocols
AGES 3 – 5 YEARS

EXPLORER ZONE (IMAGINATION TOWN)

Preschoolers develop motor coordination and imaginative roleplay through mini spiral slides, soft building foam bricks, and a miniature pretend marketplace.

  • Double wave slide with deceleration landing pads
  • Giant lightweight interlocking architectural foam bricks
  • Pretend play fire station, supermarket & ice cream van
  • 1:6 Coach-to-child supervision ratio
Book Explorer Session ($18) View Party Suites
AGES 6 – 8 YEARS

ADVENTURE ZONE (NINJA CRUISE)

High-action playground built for energetic elementary schoolers! Featuring climbing boulders, rope bridges, foam pit jumps, and a 3-level ninja crawl.

  • 14-foot traverse bouldering rock wall with safety mattresses
  • Suspended wobble balance bridges & monkey bar bridges
  • 20-foot drop spiral slide with transparent acrylic curves
  • Certified CPR/First-Aid playground marshals active
Book Adventure Session ($20) Equipment Specs
AGES 9 – 12 YEARS

CHALLENGE ZONE (SKY ARENA)

Tailored for pre-teens seeking physical challenges and teamwork! Timed agility runs, high rope traverses, air-hockey arenas, and fast-action obstacle runs.

  • Electronic timed ninja sprint obstacle course
  • Spider-web vertical climbing tower
  • Olympic grade foam jump dismount pits
  • Arcade & interactive digital target wall games
Book Challenge Session ($22) Get Unlimited Pass
